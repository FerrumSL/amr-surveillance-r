# Summary of resistance trends

Generated by `R/03_report.R` for **NCBI BioSample antibiograms (real isolates, not a surveillance sample)**.

## Key figures

- 1137 isolates after cleaning; 1137 first isolates (episode-based, 365-day episodes) from 2003-2025.
- Raw MIC and disk values interpreted with CLSI 2025; I counted as not susceptible. Percentages are only shown with at least 30 tested isolates.
- **E. coli vs ciprofloxacin**: 75% (95% CI 62-86%, n = 57) in 2013-2017, the only period with at least 30 tested isolates; 68 first isolates.
- **K. pneumoniae vs meropenem**: 72% (95% CI 65-78%, n = 192) in 2018-2022, the only period with at least 30 tested isolates; 193 first isolates.
