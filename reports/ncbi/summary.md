# Summary of resistance trends

Generated by `R/03_report.R` for **Example: public NCBI BioSample isolates (not surveillance data)**.

## Key figures

- 2004 isolates after cleaning; 2004 first isolates (episode-based, 365-day episodes) from 1998-2025.
- Raw MIC and disk values interpreted with CLSI 2025; I counted as not susceptible. Percentages are only shown with at least 30 tested isolates.
- **E. coli vs ciprofloxacin**: 84% (95% CI 79-88%, n = 231) in 2013-2017, the only period with at least 30 tested isolates; 262 first isolates.
- **K. pneumoniae vs meropenem**: 72% (95% CI 65-78%, n = 194) in 2018-2022, the only period with at least 30 tested isolates; 196 first isolates.
